## Outcome

A Components workspace: the app's own named, reusable widgets, each composed from built-in composite kinds and from other named widgets, with parameters. Operators browse, search and preview them, create and change them by voice, and use them in pages like built-in kinds.

## Depends on

App-defined composites in ui-spec/1 (requirement R1 to the ess session). Until then uilab can hold them under a document key of its own and say so.
